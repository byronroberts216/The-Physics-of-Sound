# Model definitions and independent checks

All models use SI units and reject nonfinite/nonpositive required inputs. `src/lib/physics/models.ts` is independent of React and audio. No measured room response or material data are invented.

- Traveling pressure: p/A = cos(2π(x/λ − ft)), λ=c/f, T=1/f. Rightward wave; uniform linear nondispersive air, c=343 m/s (approximate at 20°C). Displacement is −sin of the same argument, consistent with p′ ∝ −∂ξ/∂x; particle motion is bounded and visually exaggerated. OpenStax §§16.1,17.1–17.2; Penn State Wave Motion. The animation clock is slowed by a declared scale, not played at physical rate.
- Interference amplitude: sqrt(A²+B²+2AB cosφ), coherent equal-frequency sinusoids at one location. Does not add independent sound levels or imply whole-room cancellation. Derived from the superposition identity in OpenStax §§16.5,17.4.
- SPL: 20 log10(p_rms /20 μPa). Intensity level: 10 log10(I/10⁻¹² W m⁻²). Independent levels sum via 10log10(Σ10^(L/10)); this assumes uncorrelated energies and is not coherent wave addition. OpenStax §17.3 intensity definition; pressure factor of 20 follows from the square relation under fixed traveling-wave impedance (not general standing-field intensity). Test reference, doubling and stable large levels separately.
- Rigid rectangular modes: f=(c/2)sqrt[(nx/L)²+(ny/W)²+(nz/H)²]; shape ∏ cos(niπxi/Li). Nonnegative integers excluding (0,0,0). Derived by separable solutions of the homogeneous wave equation with zero normal pressure gradient at each ideal rigid wall; one-dimensional boundary condition agrees with OpenStax §17.4. REW Room Simulator describes the same rigid-boundary starting model, with additional losses our model omits. Source/listener coupling for ONE mode is product of the mode shapes, relative signed, not dB/SPL. Room modes enumeration is capped at order 6 and 200 Hz in the UI and is explicitly not a complete high-frequency prediction.
- Image source: reflect source across y=0; ray intersection follows a line from its image to listener, extra delay=(reflected length−direct length)/c. One infinite specular wall, omnidirectional point source, no diffraction or measured reflection coefficient. Distances and delay only; no sound level predicted. REW's image-source discussion supports the model context; geometry is independently derived.
- Quarter-wave depth scale: f=c/(4d), purely a wavelength/depth comparison. A rigid-wall velocity antinode in the simplest standing field occurs a quarter wavelength from the wall; this is NOT an absorber's cutoff or efficiency. Real performance depends on flow resistivity, density, angle, mounting and frequency. No Delany–Bazley coefficients or absorption ratings are implemented because primary/material evidence is not verified.
- Harmonic recipe: Σ an sin(2πnft). Illustrates integer harmonics, not the complete radiation of any named instrument. No Fourier transform of microphone audio is claimed.

Tests cover analytic cases, invalid inputs, periodicity, signs, symmetry and geometric limits. They are implementation evidence, not independent expert scientific review or validation against measured rooms.
